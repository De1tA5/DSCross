[h2]Demonshade Set Crossmod Enhancement is simple crossmod adaptation and strong enhancement for Demonshade armor[/h2]

[h3]=Mod Compatibility=[/h3]
[list]
 [*]Thorium
 [*]Secrets of the Shadows
 [*]Clicker Class
 [*]Infernal Eclipse of Ragnarok(partial)
[/list]

[h3]=Basic Functions=[/h3]
[list]
 [*]Most stats can be adjusted through configuration options
 [*]Re-added Red Devil and the Demon Flames debuff
 [*]Allows to hold Enrage key provided by mod to auto-trigger, and restores Enrage to the original 2.25×
[/list]
