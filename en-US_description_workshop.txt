[h2]Demonshade Set Crossmod Enhancement is a simple mod that enhances Demonshade armor with support for multiple mods[/h2]

[h3]=Supported Mods=[/h3]
[list]
 [*]Thorium
 [*]Secrets Of The Shadows
 [*]Clicker Class
 [*]Infernal Eclipse of Ragnarok (limited support)
[/list]

[h3]=Features=[/h3]
[list]
 [*]Most stats can be adjusted in the configuration menu
 [*]Additional visual effects while wearing Demonshade armor
 [*]Restores the Red Devil and the Demon Flames debuff
 [*]Hold the Demonshade Enrage key to activate it automatically, with an option to revert the Demonshade Enrage nerf
[/list]

[h3]=Planned Updates=[/h3]
[list]
 [*]Support for Homeward Journey
 [*]Additional enhancements for Gem Tech armor
 [*]Developer items
[/list]
[spoiler]These plans are not guaranteed to be implemented[/spoiler]
